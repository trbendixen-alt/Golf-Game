# Credits

## Art and assets

**No third-party art, models, textures, fonts, sounds or packs are used in the game so far.**
(Kenney.nl and other free asset packs were considered for Milestone 7 but not needed, and
nothing was downloaded.) If any are added later, list each one here with its exact source
and licence before it is committed.

Everything you see in *Main Street Opener* is generated by code in this repo:

| What | How it is made | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings, cars, trees, lamp posts, cones, fences, hills, barn, silos, water tower | Simple boxes / cylinders / spheres built in code, merged or instanced | `golf-game/tools/build_hole_scenery.gd`, `tools/mesh_builder.gd` |
| Windows, facades, glint on glass | Procedural shader (no textures) | `golf-game/shaders/town/building_common.gdshaderinc` |
| Street, sidewalk, fairway, rough, green, farmland | Procedural shader (no textures) | `golf-game/shaders/town/ground.gdshader` |
| Sky, sun, clouds | Procedural sky shader | `golf-game/shaders/town/sky.gdshader` |
| Tree leaves swaying, flag rippling | Procedural shaders | `golf-game/shaders/town/foliage.gdshader`, `flag.gdshader` |
| Sun-shadow / ambient-occlusion mask | Baked by our own tool into one small image | `golf-game/scenes/holes/main_street_opener/sun_mask.png` |

## Fonts

Both fonts are free and open-licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows use in commercial
games). They were downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository
(https://github.com/google/fonts) and are stored, unmodified except where noted, in
`golf-game/fonts/` next to their licence text.

| Font | Used for | Source | Licence | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lilita One** by Juan Montoreano | Title, buttons | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lilitaone | SIL OFL 1.1 | `fonts/lilita_one/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf`, `OFL.txt` |
| **Fredoka** (variable font) by The Fredoka Project Authors | Chips, tagline, body text | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka | SIL OFL 1.1 | `fonts/fredoka/Fredoka-Variable.ttf` (renamed from `Fredoka[wdth,wght].ttf`), `OFL.txt` |

The menu's button images (`golf-game/ui/theme/btn_*.png`) are drawn by our own tool
(`tools/build_ui_theme.gd`), and the menu background is a render of our own Main Street scene
(`tools/render_menu_background.gd`). No other third-party assets were added.

## Code we build on

- **Julian Crowe's look test** (`golf-game/look_test/`, this repo, added in pull request #1):
  the golden-hour look, sky, building, foliage, flag and ground shaders, and the lighting
  numbers in `data/looks.json`. The files in `shaders/town/` are adapted copies of his
  shaders (the building shader now reads its settings from the mesh so buildings can be
  merged; the ground shader uses the baked shadow mask). His originals are left untouched.
- **Godot Engine** (https://godotengine.org), MIT licence. The default UI font is the
  one bundled with Godot; see Godot's own licence notices for it.
